ORIGIN

High above the mist line at 2,100 meters, our tea gardens breathe crystal Alpine air and morning cloud dew. The extreme diurnal temperature shifts concentrate essential polyphenols, yielding notes of wild orchid, roasted pine, and liquid gold honey.

Every harvest adheres strictly to the biodynamic calendar, plucked exclusively during the dawn dew window to preserve the cellular integrity of the leaves.
